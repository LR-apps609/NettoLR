Privacy

Effective 28 October 2026.

In short

Your data stays on your iPhone. NettoLR has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Your profile (optional name, job, ATECO code, VAT number if you enter it), income, payments and the F24 receipts you add: saved in the app's storage, protected by your iPhone passcode.
  • Photos of your certificate and invoices are read on the phone, without internet, and not saved. Photos of F24 receipts are saved only if you add them.
  • Reminders are notifications scheduled on the phone.
  • Face ID is handled by iOS: NettoLR only learns “unlocked” or “not unlocked”.

Backups

If you make a backup, you choose where to save it (for example iCloud Drive). From there it's kept by that service, under its own rules.

Subscription

Purchases go through Apple's App Store. NettoLR never sees your payment details: it only knows whether your subscription is active.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no ads. Fonts and styles are part of the site's own files.

Deleting everything

In “You” there's “Delete all data”. Deleting the app also removes everything in it.
