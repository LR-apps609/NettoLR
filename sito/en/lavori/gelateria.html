Ice cream shop with an Italian VAT number: what you keep

Gelaterie e pasticcerie. Also known as: ice cream shop, pastry shop, bakery.

Bring in €30,000 in a year and you keep about

€24,280

Yours €24,280Pension €4,612Taxes €1,108

For the first five years, if it's your first business, taxes drop to €369.

What to know

Your percentage is 40%. On the flat-rate scheme (forfettario) you don't count expenses: of every €100 you bring in, €40 is the income you pay on.

Pension: you pay into the INPS Traders scheme (a fixed amount, plus a share above the minimum).

Taxes: a single tax of 15%, or 5% for the first 5 years.

Up to €85,000 of income a year you can stay on the flat-rate scheme.
